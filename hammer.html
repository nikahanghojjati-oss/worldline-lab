HOW HAMMER EARNS A CHANGE

Not a vote. A physical discriminator.

  1. Specify both ontologies.Build genuine competing one-history and many-history physical models with the same feasible apparatus.
  2. Predict different records.Derive separate likelihoods from the actual equations, not a hypothetical adjustable knob.
  3. Test the world.Use calibrated original data, real uncertainties and an adversarial nuisance model.
  4. Reproduce and review.Version the calculations and use genuinely invoked, source-studying AI model critics.
  5. Revise transparently.Record why the central evidence genuinely prefers one model; preserve alternatives and reversal criteria.

Until these gates are met, HAMMER can still advance model-specific Ledger findings without fabricating a central shift.

THE HAMMER SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Intuition, checked by experiment.

01

Study

Read primary-source theory, reproduce methods, record what remains unknown.

02

Invent

Combine incompatible-seeming questions into physically coherent candidate experiments.

03

Challenge

Seek the strongest rival explanation, shared-noise confounder and no-go theorem.

04

Measure

Identify a feasible observable; compare theory with original controlled observations.

05

Learn

Preserve errors, uncertainty, negative tests and genuinely verified improvements.

06

Decide

Move a model-specific claim—or the central Compass when evidence truly separates histories.
