# S-002-R006 · Ten source-complete main-text investigations, chosen for empirical discrimination

Date 2026-10-21. This batch prioritizes measured collapse-model exclusions and tests that expose weaknesses in an alleged unique discriminator. A *full main-text critical study* means the original article's main sections, methods, results, figures and conclusions were examined at source level, with alternatives and limits below; independent replication of supplements, raw datasets or every proof is NOT claimed. Selection rank is expert prioritization, not calculated probability of moving the central compass.

The original works are not interchangeable: molecule fringe scans, Gran Sasso photon counts and LISA mechanical noises are different observables. Dai and Altamura **share LISA mission records**; their separate theoretical analyses are not independent experimental replications. Cosmological and gravitational-entanglement papers are model-specific **theoretical** filters, not new physical branch detections.

## 1. SRC-048 — Fein et al. 2019
Source: https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~gillrd/Peking/Files/two-slit_expt.pdf

**Sections actually studied:** Original six-page Nature Physics letter, pp1-4 Methods, controls, Figs1-3, pp5-6 scholarly appendix/references.

**Question/result:** Measured KDTLI fringes from 26,777-Da most abundant molecules in 2m LUMI apparatus; single scan V=25±3%, normalized quantum-vs-measured fit 0.93±0.06, macroscopicity 14.1.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Finite velocity distribution, grating drift/roll and residual environment; raw fringe events and supplementary data not rerun.

**Ledger impact:** Direct experiment narrows strong objective-collapse rates at tested mass/separation; not proof of real Everett histories.

## 2. SRC-067 — Dai/Miao/Ma 2024
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.17588

**Sections actually studied:** Full seven-page arXiv v1 main text §§I–IV, measured-noise figures 1-2, projected underground Fig3, supporting bibliography.

**Question/result:** LISA Pathfinder 2017 Brownian acceleration uncertainty 0.075 fm²/s⁴/Hz supports authors' CSL λ<8.3e-11 /s at rC=1e-7m; DP cutoff >285.5 fm.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Projected 3e-11/s underground sensitivity is NOT measured; shared LISA mission data, Brownian gas noise model, calculations not re-run.

**Ledger impact:** Real measured-data reanalysis constraints on specified collapse parameters, not observed collapse.

## 3. SRC-068 — Altamura/Vinante/Carlesso 2025
Source: https://iris.cnr.it/bitstream/20.500.14243/536713/1/2501.08971v1.pdf

**Sections actually studied:** Original entire seven-page arXiv author v1 §§I–V, physical noise Fig1/2 and Appendix A geometry; journal PRA 111 L020203.

**Question/result:** LISA 1.928kg 4.6cm cubes; minimum measured torque spectral density 5.7e-34 N²m²/Hz at 3mHz; ~2-fold tighter CSL upper bound for rC 10^-5.5 to 10^-3.5m.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** One reanalysis of existing LISA data, not a new independent mission; potential residual gas and electrode correlations; original measured angular spectrum not refit.

**Ledger impact:** Constrained rotational-noise collapse parameter domain with geometry-specific limits; cannot count branches.

## 4. SRC-069 — Donadi et al. 2021
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09556-0

**Sections actually studied:** Entire original EPJC HTML §§1–4 and §3.1–3.3 statistical fit, figs1-3, references and data availability.

**Question/result:** 124kg-day Ge detector at LNGS, 1000-3800keV, atomic coherent nuclear radiation scales Z² versus electrons Z; authors report factor-13 more stringent CSL bound at 95% credibility from modeled Geant4 background.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Uniform-prior Bayesian posterior is not automatically frequentist confidence; 2026 xenon low-energy signal physics differs; raw Ge simulation/event data unavailable to our replication.

**Ledger impact:** Independent real detector constraint on the specified white-noise CSL spectrum, not Everett evidence.

## 5. SRC-070 — Carlesso/Ferialdi/Bassi 2018
Source: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1140/epjd/e2018-90248-x.pdf

**Sections actually studied:** Full original seven-page European Physical Journal D PDF §§1–5 and Fig1 frequency-cutoff exclusion plots.

**Question/result:** Drude-Lorentz colored CSL S(ω)=Swhite Ωc²/(Ωc²+ω²). Original main article compares mechanical, x-ray and phonon/heat bounds using cutoffs 10^1,10^4,10^15 and infinity per second.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Derives reanalysis of earlier measurements, no new independent physical detector, curves not numerically reproduced.

**Ledger impact:** A low colored-noise cutoff suppresses high-frequency radiation predictions, so a white-noise exclusion cannot be called exclusion of every colored variant.

## 6. SRC-071 — Martin/Vennin 2021
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09290-7

**Sections actually studied:** Complete EPJC HTML original §§1–5 with Fourier modes, collapse criterion R(k), power spectra, Gaussianity and conclusions.

**Question/result:** Within their cosmological CSL field formalism, even-parity collapse operators fail to generate collapse; odd higher orders cause non-Gaussian issues, yielding a preference for linear field operators. Some constant-parameter CMB examples require λ<1e-73/s at specified inflation energy.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Running curvature-dependent λ could evade tension; relativity completion and actual detector CMB likelihood not independently rerun.

**Ledger impact:** Model-class theoretical restriction involving observed CMB Gaussianity; not observed physically separate histories.

## 7. SRC-072 — Aziz/Howl 2025
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09595-7

**Sections actually studied:** Original Nature complete open HTML main sections on QFT classical gravity, Feynman masses, Eq4, Fig4 and Methods.

**Question/result:** Quantum-matter virtual propagators can create entanglement through a classical gravitational background in the formulated local QFT; entanglement alone is not always a unique graviton witness. Distinct mass/time scales permit experimental test designs.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Theoretical counterexample, not experiment; depends on assumed classical field coupling and regime and can be separated with apparatus design.

**Ledger impact:** Strong adversarial validity correction for a proposed gravity witness, not one-vs-many ontology result.

## 8. SRC-014 — Frauchiger/Renner 2018
Source: https://boulderschool.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/QConsistency.pdf

**Sections actually studied:** Complete published main theoretical argument in institutional 19-page author PDF (intro, Q/C/S assumptions, four agents, tables1–4, theorem and discussion); extensive appendices not fully replicated.

**Question/result:** Wigner-friend style nested certainty statements contradict if their precise quantum applicability, unique outcomes and cross-agent consistency assumptions are jointly imposed.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** SRC-064 already full critically reviewed disputes theorem's broad interpretation via complete Bohmian global state; no experiment of nested macroscopic coherent agents.

**Ledger impact:** Conditional no-go theorem deepens scope of one-world inference but not proof that Everett branching physically occurs.

## 9. SRC-073 — Bedingham/Maroney 2017
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10701-017-0084-5

**Sections actually studied:** Open access full Foundations of Physics main §§1–6, collapse jump operator identities, lattice/QMUPL dynamics and discussion.

**Question/result:** If jump and unitary operators admit a common symmetry basis, a collapse-event history can have a time-symmetric formulation with asymmetric boundary conditions.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Boundary-state premises, reversibility of event law differs from subjective arrow of memory, lattice numerical ensemble not independently recreated.

**Ledger impact:** Refutes overbroad assumption all stochastic collapse laws must fundamentally violate time reversal; no observation of time loops.

## 10. SRC-074 — te Vrugt/Tóth/Wittkowski 2021
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10825-021-01804-6

**Sections actually studied:** Entire open original main §§1–9: Wigner/Moyal formalism, GRW/CSL/DP/dissipative master generators, Fokker-Planck approximations and molecular dynamics.

**Question/result:** Authors' reported simulation of anti-thermodynamic initial states found GRW noise did NOT restore thermodynamic behavior as predicted by one Albert-style argument.

**Alternative explanations, sensitivity and non-replication:** Simulation is not experiment; not every GRW parameterization ruled out; no independent MD code rerun.

**Ledger impact:** Specific proposed GRW explanation of thermodynamic irreversibility weakened; experimental ledger remains separately constrained.
