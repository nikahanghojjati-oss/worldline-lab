## Method and counting gate
A **full study** here means examining the complete **main original publication or scholarly entry** (all its principal sections, methods/argument, results, discussion and conclusions), recording methodological assumptions, important contrary interpretations and the question it can/cannot answer. This does **not** claim independent replication of every tensor proof, raw experimental record, supplementary calculation or all works in a cited bibliography. Where an article has external supplementary data, those may require additional independent audit. Each separate resource is counted once; existing abstract and partial notes are retained as historical stages. No source has been double-counted across fields. This precise definition is documented to avoid confusing a critical literature review with replication or newly performed experiment.

**Main scientific question:** Does our actual universe have one physically actual history or multiple real quantum histories? A time-loop solution, causal-order circuit or inflationary bubble is **not** automatically an Everett branch.

**Independent crosschecks actually performed:** (1) CHSH singlet settings: 2√2≈2.828427, compared with 2 local bound (a separate inequality from Bell's own 1964 Eq. 22); (2) PBR Eq. 1 two-qubit mutually orthonormal measurement vectors and excluded preparation overlaps, matrix residual below 5×10⁻¹⁶; (3) Gödel circular timelike criterion r>asinh(1)≈0.881374; (4) Oreshkov ideal task 0.853553>0.75; (5) Proietti 0.416/0.075≈5.55 standard deviations. These are limited model calculations, not reproductions of laboratory samples.

## Ten study reports

### Study 01 · Bell — On the Einstein Podolsky Rosen paradox (1964)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://journals.aps.org/ppf/pdf/10.1103/PhysicsPhysiqueFizika.1.195).
- **Coverage certified:** Complete original six-page paper, Physics 1, 195–200, §§I–VI including examples, quantitative contradiction, generalization and conclusion.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** The singlet predicts E(a,b)=−a·b. A hidden-variable average of local preassigned A(a,λ), B(b,λ) with setting-independent response and a probability distribution for λ cannot reproduce all quantum spin correlations. Bell constructs one-spin hidden-variable and simpler correlation examples first to show why the locality condition—not the mere existence of hidden variables—drives the contradiction.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Traced the normalization/perfect anticorrelation condition to Bell’s restriction on response functions, his inequality in §IV and the angle-smoothed non-approximation argument. Separately checked the related CHSH singlet configuration: four coplanar axes yield |S|=2√2≈2.828>2. This CHSH crosscheck is NOT a rederivation of Bell’s specific equation (22).
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Original theorem relies on locality and independence of measurement choices from λ, and on identifying outcomes/setting response appropriately; later experiments face statistical and detection assumptions. Bell does not forbid explicitly nonlocal Bohmian dynamics; the abstract claim that all hidden variables were ruled out is incorrect.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Bohm’s 1952 nonlocal theory, and the carefully different assumptions of 2015 loophole-free Bell tests; examine outcome independence, parameter independence and superdeterminism debates next.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Strong theorem against specified local hidden-variable accounts, ZERO direct proof that another physically real quantum history exists.

### Study 02 · Pusey–Barrett–Rudolph — On the reality of the quantum state (2012)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.3328).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire eight-page arXiv manuscript: definitions and toy example pp.1–2, n-system construction and noise bound pp.2–4, conclusion pp.4–5, Appendix A pp.5–6, Appendix B pp.7–8, Appendix C p.8.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** Define an ontic microstate λ prepared according to μψ(λ); overlapping support for different pure quantum states encodes a ψ-epistemic picture. Assuming independent preparations produce independent physical states, n copies yield a nonzero chance of a common overlap for all 2^n preparation combinations. A joint exclusion measurement assigns a zero-probability outcome to each such combination, contradicting an overlap model.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Directly checked the four two-qubit exclusion states of Eq.(1) against |00>, |0+>, |+0>, |++>: each forbidden inner product is numerically zero (residual <3×10^−17); the four measurement vectors are orthonormal (residual <5×10^−16). Examined Appendix A n-system circuit, Appendix B overlap/total-variation noise bound and Appendix C numerical semidefinite-program discussion; the general circuit and optimization were not independently reconstructed.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Preparation independence and ontic-state sufficiency are substantive assumptions. Paper tests whether the wavefunction is 'mere information' about λ in a defined model family—not the existence or count of Everett branches. Cannot infer many worlds from ψ-ontology.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Preparation-correlated models, contextual responses, ontology distinctions in Bohmian and Everett interpretations; seek a recent critical reconstruction of theorem assumptions.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Rigorous, assumption-scoped constraint on ψ-epistemic models; zero direct history-multiplicity evidence.

### Study 03 · Feeney et al. — First Observational Tests of Eternal Inflation (2011)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1012.1995).
- **Coverage certified:** Complete five-page original paper: theory/templates, simulation/data selection, needlets and Hough edge detection, patch-wise Bayesian evidence, WMAP results and references.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** Some eternal-inflation models produce observable bubble-collision perturbations with axial symmetry and a causal boundary, modeled by angular temperature profiles. Their pipeline tests for candidate patches and circular edges in foreground-masked WMAP W-band data, then assesses evidence ratios for collision templates vs ΛCDM.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Checked 94-GHz W-band choice, 70.6% unmasked sky, noise/foreground simulation logic, needlet significance thresholds, edge detector and MultiNest patch-evidence prior choices. Paper reports 15 blobs minus four masked = 11 evaluated; strongest inference posterior peaks at zero mean detectable collisions, with mean number <1.6 at 68% credibility. The original analysis code/maps were not rerun.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Bound is on detectable collision signals under assumed priors, template, masking and survey sensitivity, NOT on the total number of inaccessible bubble regions. An arXiv PDF may be later recompiled; 2011 publication and source model provenance are distinct. Inflation bubbles are ontologically different from Everett branches.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Foregrounds, four somewhat anomalous patches, later Planck-era data and alternate collision templates; require fresh full-survey reproduction to update cosmological verdict.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Model-conditional null CMB result; neither establishes nor eliminates a cosmological multiverse and cannot identify quantum branch count.

### Study 04 · Vaidman — Many-Worlds Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics (SEP, revised 2026)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-manyworlds/).
- **Coverage certified:** Full scholarly entry, revised 17 June 2026, §§1–8 including definitions, macroscopic state mapping, preferred basis, probability, nonlocality, tests, explicit objections, conclusion and bibliography overview.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** MWI treats the universal quantum state as evolving without fundamental collapse, and defines experienced macroscopic worlds as approximate correlated components. It distinguishes the mathematical single global state from empirical experiences of many definite-looking worlds. It outlines Born-weight/post-measurement self-location, decoherence as preferred-basis motivation and disagreements over probability and explanatory adequacy.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Read all eight sections, including the world's approximate/FAPP status, orthogonal decomposition ∑αᵢ|worldᵢ>, the Born-weight postulate, proposed macroscopic interference tests, criticisms and the author's reply. Critically separated authorial advocacy from uncontested physics and from direct experiments. Checked logical counterpoint that Bohmian mechanics may predict identical tested statistics with one actual configuration.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Scholarly interpretive overview, not an experimental research paper and no direct measurement of branch ontology. World individuation and observer-weight probability are debated; the text itself notes realism tests against Bohm may be infeasible. Full bibliography was indexed as context, not every cited work separately studied.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Bohmian definite positions, GRW physical collapse and critiques by Kent, Albert and Hemmo/Shenker; full original studies remain valuable.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Full scholarly resource examined; establishes the structure and controversies of MWI, not empirical plurality of realized histories.

### Study 05 · Oreshkov–Costa–Brukner — Quantum correlations with no causal order (2012)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3493644/).
- **Coverage certified:** Complete open-access journal main article: problem setup, causal inequality, quantum instruments and process matrices, nonseparable example, classical limit, discussion and methods (PMC main text). Supplementary derivations separately flagged not independently replicated.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** In a binary communication task under a definite causal order and independently chosen bits, one direction cannot transmit information and success ≤3/4. If quantum operations remain valid *locally* but no global definite order is assumed, process matrices allow causally nonseparable correlations. Their explicit two-party process exceeds the causal inequality.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Traced closed-lab/free-choice assumptions, CPTP/CJ machinery and normalization, and the example in their Eqs.(7)–(8). Checked the illustrative maximum (2+√2)/4≈0.853553 against 0.75, a gap of ≈0.10355. This numerical check is not a full independent proof of positivity of their process matrix or the supplementary classification.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** A mathematically permitted process with no presupposed global causal order is not an observed gravitational spacetime, and is not proof of physically branching outcomes. Supplementary formalization remains a specific unreplicated component.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Quantum-switch optical implementations have different operational assumptions from this causal-inequality model; avoid claiming those experiments directly test the Oreshkov inequality.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Formal expansion of admissible causal structures, neutral on one-versus-many actual quantum histories.

### Study 06 · Procopio et al. — Experimental superposition of orders of quantum gates (2015)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4918346/).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire open-access journal main text, theory/commutation task, interferometer configuration, all Pauli-gate and 100 random-pair results, discussion and Methods subsections. Separate supplementary PDF identified, not independently reproduced.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** A photon's path coherently controls the order of two polarization gates in a Mach–Zehnder-type interferometer. With the promise that gates commute or anticommute, measurement of the control can identify the relation with one use of each gate, surpassing the success ceiling for fixed-order protocols under the stated query model.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Read gate-use operational definition, 16 Pauli combinations, 50 commuting and 50 anticommuting random cases, optical apparatus, results and noise controls. Reported random-pair correctness 0.976±0.015 vs fixed-order probabilistic bound 0.9288 and Pauli-case rate 0.973±0.016. Algebra sanity check: XZ=−ZX and IX=XI; interference control can encode the sign. Did not independently rerun photon counts or derive the supplementary quantum-comb optimum.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Gate order in an interferometer is an operational quantum-circuit resource in one laboratory spacetime; not a human returning to another history or evidence of coexisting macroscopic worlds. Fixed-order comparison rests on carefully specified allowed queries and promises.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Later discussions distinguish causally nonseparable operations from indefinite metric or globally indefinite spacetime; preserve this boundary in lab visuals.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Strong quantum-information experiment, zero empirical branch-census impact.

### Study 07 · Proietti et al. — Experimental test of local observer-independence (2019)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.05080).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire original main paper pp.1–5 including setup, inequality, six-photon methods, 64 measurement settings, results, assumptions and discussion; supporting technical appendices pp.5–10 inspected for fusion-gate transformations and analysis framework but raw tables not recomputed.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** Models each Wigner-friend record using a photonic degree of freedom and tests a Bell-type inequality encoding assumptions of locality, free choice and observer-independent facts. This is explicitly not a direct observation of another world. Reported S=2.416±0.075 based on 1,794 sixfold events over 360 hours, a violation >5 standard deviations under the stated model.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Traced memory-photon entanglement, type-I fusion gates, choice of observables, coincidence counting and statistical assumption of Poissonian noise. Checked the numerical significance (2.416−2)/0.075≈5.55. Appendix fusion-gate heralding probability and measurement formalism were inspected but no independent raw-data reanalysis performed.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Authors explicitly discuss fair-sampling, spacetime, observer-memory observables and postselection caveats. Calling a small photonic record 'a human observer' would overreach. They show tension in a conjunction of assumptions, not a proof that Everett histories physically exist.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Single-world nonlocal Bohmian models, collapse alternatives and philosophy of observer definition; reanalyze complete probability tables before further experimental attribution.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Conditional observer-independent-facts result, neutral on realized global history count.

### Study 08 · Lazarovici–Hubert — How Quantum Mechanics can consistently describe the use of itself (2019)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-37535-1).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire 2019 Scientific Reports open-access research article from introduction through extended-FR derivation, analysis of effective collapse, Bohmian framework, relativistic nonlocality, objections and concluding perspective; references/author metadata checked.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** Responding to Frauchiger–Renner's claimed conflict among universal quantum validity, unique outcomes and cross-agent consistency, this paper tracks the entire laboratory quantum state through the sequence. A single definite Bohmian configuration can coexist with empty wave components that later recohere. Invalid intermediate inference comes from treating a locally effectively collapsed state as a fundamental collapse even as future Wigner-type measurements revive interference.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Followed the heads/tails initial weights, t1/t2 state expansion, Wigner measurements, outcome inference and separate discussion of why unobserved environment records invalidate the idealized reversal. Rechecked logically that different observers' local state assignments cannot replace a complete subsystem-plus-environment analysis. Full laboratory Bohmian particle trajectory simulation not reproduced.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Counterargument is theory-specific and contests interpretation of a theorem; it does not experimentally prove Bohmian ontology. Macroscopic recoil/recoherence assumed ideal; relativistic foliation and nonlocality tensions remain. Original Frauchiger–Renner full-paper reconstruction remains a next-phase countercheck.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** FR 2018 paper, Everett relative states, objective-collapse dynamics, quantum logical frameworks; avoid labeling this rebuttal a consensus refutation.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Directly demonstrates a formulated single-world response to an influential paradox. Reduces grounds for treating paradox as forced evidence of multiple realities; no numerical shift.

### Study 09 · Gödel — Cosmological rotating solution (1949)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/pdf/10.1103/RevModPhys.21.447).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire original four-page paper pp.447–450: nine claimed properties, metric/field-equation construction, explicit timelike circles, homogeneous-space interpretation, and concluding observational caveats.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** An exact homogeneous rotating dust solution with cosmological constant is time orientable but does not admit a global time coordinate increasing on all timelike curves. In cylindrical coordinates, a constant-r circle becomes timelike beyond sinh r=1, so a closed timelike worldline is permitted by this particular metric.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Examined metric/Einstein-equation setup, rotating frame properties and the explicit criterion. Independently factored the constant-radius angular coefficient proportional to sinh²r(sinh²r−1), giving r>asinh(1)=ln(1+√2)≈0.881374. This verifies the local timelike-circle sign but not all tensor field equations, homogeneous group claims or cosmological modeling.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** Gödel expressly notes the model lacks cosmological redshift. Physical observations do not identify our actual universe with this rotating metric. Closed curves in a model do not entail multiple Everett histories or travel between branches; chronologically unusual spacetime still contains one mathematical geometry.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Observed expansion, chronology protection and modern cosmological isotropy constraints; compare assumptions before translating topology to physical reality.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Exact classical counterexample to universal global-time ordering in GR, zero empirical evidence for many actual outcome branches.

### Study 10 · Hawking — Chronology protection conjecture (1992)

- **Original primary resource:** [full text](https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/~loeb/Hawking_C.pdf).
- **Coverage certified:** Entire nine-page Phys Rev D paper pp.603–611: introduction, compact-horizon geometry, classical energy restrictions, closed null geodesic boost/area, topology argument, semiclassical Misner-space field state, quantum backreaction speculation and conclusion.
- **Central argument and material actually followed:** For the formation of causality-violating regions in a finite predictable spacetime domain, Hawking analyzes compactly generated future Cauchy horizons. Under a noncompact initial surface and suitable energy condition, the horizon is obstructed; semiclassical field stress near nearly closed null curves may further destabilize attempts to create such a horizon.
- **Methods and independently checked aspects:** Reviewed the complete geometric route from horizon generators and Raychaudhuri/Newman–Penrose energy restriction to Misner-space image-state stress and the final Planck-scale cutoff/backreaction assessment. Crosschecked earlier notes NOTE-016/022 and later Kay–Radzikowski–Wald / Visser arguments. The tensor manipulations and stress renormalization have NOT been independently rederived.
- **Uncertainty / methodological limitations:** A conjecture with theorem-like conditional pieces, not a rigorous universal quantum-gravity theorem. Hawking explicitly lacked proof for all quantum states on Misner space; horizon assumptions are narrower than arbitrary eternally existing CTC spacetime. No direct observation of time machine formation occurs.
- **Adversarial comparator and next reproduction:** Kay–Radzikowski–Wald's Hadamard breakdown and Visser's semiclassical reliability critique; both refine the meaning of 'divergence' and prediction failure.
- **Effect on actual one-history vs many-history question:** Theory limits attempted past-directed time travel but provides no direct discrimination between one actually realized quantum history and many.

## Cross-paper synthesis and no-change audit
Bell and PBR are strong conditional theorems against identified model classes. The many-worlds reference is a comprehensive interpretive defense, not evidence of an individually observed extra branch. Gödel and Hawking concern global causal geometry and its hypothesized physical accessibility, not a multiplicity of quantum outcome histories. Oreshkov/Procopio demonstrate formal and operational quantum causal-order structures, again not a count of histories. Proietti tests a particular conjunction of locality, choice and observer-independent records without uniquely singling out Everettian ontology; Lazarovici/Hubert provide a directly competing noncollapse, single-actual-configuration account. Feeney's CMB null test concerns cosmological bubble signals rather than quantum Everett histories. 

**Verdict:** There is no independently demonstrated experimental comparison that establishes exactly how many simultaneously actual Everett-type histories exist. Existing **59/100 remains an editorial visualization, not a calibrated posterior probability.** Audit **VC-007 = 59→59 (no change)**. The full-study count will rise due to genuine critical reading, not because the universe has been proven one or many.

**Next gates:** deeper replication of experimental raw tables/controls, PBR noise-constrained exclusion proof, full independent Hawking metric-stress analysis, observational-equivalence matrix for Bohm vs Everett vs constrained collapse models, and complete review of Frauchiger–Renner original as a countercheck to SRC-064. The full-studies count is literature work completed, not the percentage of ontology established.
