## Review 12 · SRC-059 · David Bohm (1952), Part II

Primary full facsimile https://cqi.inf.usi.ch/qic/bohm2.pdf ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.180 (Physical Review 85,180–193).

Coverage: Entire original article, §§1–10 measurement and observing apparatus dynamics; pointer packet separation and effective result, measurement limitations, EPR correlated particles, von Neumann hidden-variable theorem objections, conclusion, Appendices A and B on electromagnetic field and objections.

Method: Correlate microscopic states with macroscopically separated pointer wave packets through apparatus interaction; single actual configuration occupies one packet even while unoccupied wavefunction branches remain in ψ. Quantum equilibrium gives standard Born readout probabilities. EPR analysis requires nonlocal influences in configuration-space dynamics; therefore Bell's later locality inequality is not an impossibility theorem for the model.

Critical limits: Ideal nonoverlapping packets are approximations; no independently solved detector dynamics, full appendical electromagnetic field calculation or relativistically invariant extension. Hypothetical subquantum measurement improvements rely on *modified* physical assumptions, not equilibrium Bohm's standard predictions.

Opposition: The globally coexisting Everett relative branches versus Bohm's single occupied configuration differ in ontology but not ordinary tested frequencies. Objective-collapse proposals produce distinct parameter-conditioned loss.

Outcome: Fully studied main text plus appendices, NOTE-044. No direct history count.

## Review 13 · SRC-065 · B. Hensen et al. (2016), Bell spin experiment 2 and additional analysis

Complete published open-access main article https://www.nature.com/articles/srep30289 ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/srep30289, Scientific Reports 6,30289, published 2016-08-11.

Coverage: Abstract, introductory prior-Bell-run context, trial selection/event-ready entanglement description and both ψ+ and ψ− heralding, second run's CHSH analysis, conventional vs complete p-values, combination of two experiments under distinct assumptions, random number generation bias/early-bits derivation and null-variable conditional-independence assumptions, discussion/methods and conclusions. Source includes full text through displayed mathematical Eq.35; separate supplements/raw detector records not independently processed.

Quantitative original results: Second run 300 trials obtained in 210 hours across 22 days, CHSH S₂=2.35±0.18. Conventional p=0.029 (Gaussian/ideal independence assumptions) versus complete p=0.061 (arbitrary memory, partly predictable settings). Joint 545 trials give S_combined=2.38±0.136, with complete p=0.017 for independent-run Fisher combination or p=0.0080 if both treated as one pooled test. These are NOT the same test or the 2015 first-run values. A crude (S−2)/0.18≈1.94 is only a consistency arithmetic check and is NOT the paper's rigorous tail probability.

Critical limits: Local hidden-variable rejection is conditional on space-like timing and RNG independence (with quantified early-bit tolerance); second run alone complete p=.061 is greater than .05; full raw event-count and bias simulations not repeated. Nonlocal Bohmian mechanics is compatible with Bell violations; Everett shares standard joint probabilities.

Opposition: Model-history ontology cannot be chosen using a test targeting local-realist factorization. The source supports quantum nonlocality constraints, not multiple Everett-world detection.

Outcome: Entire main work critically reviewed, NOTE-045, not first 2015 run (SRC-060) and not an independently replicated experiment.
