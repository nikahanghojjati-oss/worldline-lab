# Worldline Lab · S-002-R009 · One history or multiple histories: evidence-first discriminator strategy

Date: 2026-10-22. Owner direction: all research and the Truth Compass should revolve around **does our real universe have one physically realized outcome history or multiple physically real histories?** Not the viability of time travel, the number of articles catalogued, generic quantum interest, or experimentally untestable slogans.

## 1. A directional meter that can legitimately move

Treat 50 as the neutral editorial **starting point**, not a scientific 50% posterior. Before admitting any directional score proposal, compare **fully specified physical versions** rather than words:
* E: Everettian universal unitary dynamics with physically real emergent branches and Born weight rule;
* B_eq: pilot-wave Bohmian mechanics with one actual configuration, `rho(q,t0)=|psi(q,t0)|²`, and same ordinary Born measurement predictions;
* B_neq: **additional speculative premise** that specific preparations/cosmological relics have `rho != |psi|²`; **not** equivalent to standard Bohmian equilibrium;
* C(theta): objective collapse such as a specified mass-proportional white-noise CSL parameter point; macroscopic records become physically unique under modified stochastic law.

For ordinary Born outcomes, under matched preparation/measurement conditions, **P(data|E)=P(data|B_eq)**. Thus likelihood ratio E/B_eq equals 1, regardless of sample size. An experiment that disfavors *specific* C(theta) can indirectly change E's **normalized share among E, B_eq and C(theta)** if a **declared prior** allocated support to C(theta). It cannot change E/B_eq odds. Reallocating excluded-collapse plausibility entirely to E would be misleading. A central directional reading *could* move in a fully specified, model-averaged score **conditional on documented priors, a prior over CSL parameters, nuisance/background uncertainties and model completeness**. Those priors and the parameter marginalization are not scientifically fixed at present. Therefore do NOT advertise the raw conditional posterior as a discovered percentage or silently adopt it as the canonical needle.

## 2. Ranked research campaigns strictly tied to main question

### Gate 1 · MOST RELEVANT: Can any observed record discriminate physically actual branches from one configuration?

**Original work to produce:** formal equivalence/identifiability proof for E vs B_eq for an explicit instrument (Stern–Gerlach, quantum eraser, decohering pointer) and complete source-equation comparison, with predictions `P_E(k)=P_Beq(k)`. If equal for the chosen domain, certify *NO DISCRIMINATOR* and remove that entire conventional experiment class from the high-priority reading queue. Search for precise extra assumptions that make a measurable difference; require not just an interpretation name but distribution `P(k|H1)` vs `P(k|H2)`. **Neither side can get a point from an inherently equal-likelihood test.** Everett original SRC-007, Bohm I & II SRC-010/059, opponent sources from Valentini 2008 https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.0810 and its later counter-replies. Opponent critique to be read before making ontological predictions.

### Gate 2 · HIGH: pilot-wave nonequilibrium as a possible empirical one-history discriminator

**Source candidates:** Valentini & Varma (Phys Rev D 112,112024, published 30 Dec 2025, https://doi.org/10.1103/4llk-pt1j ; accessible author version https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07510), and Valentini (2024) https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10782. Target experiments: spin/polarization deviations in short-lived high-energy collision products. Target observable: deviations from Bloch/Born linearity of expectation vs detector angle and standard production distributions, with quantitative background detector calibration and conventional beyond-SM rivals. A repeatable `rho neq |psi|²` deviation matching a preregistered pilot-wave nonequilibrium distribution could **directly constrain Born-level universal unitary E or favour specified B_neq**, conditional on other modified quantum dynamical models. A null bounds that B_neq parameter family but does NOT refute B_eq. **Paper is a future test proposal, not existing confirmed Born violation.**

### Gate 3 · HIGH: objective collapse versus unitarity with progressively macroscopic controlled recoherence

Use SRC-048 heavy-molecule 2019 interferometry, original XENONnT 2026 SRC-066, Ge SRC-069, gravity-mechanics SRC-067/068 and genuine collapse-candidate GRW 1986 SRC-042. Pre-register spatial separation, mass density, time, environmental decoherence, predicted recoherence/visibility under C(theta) vs E/B_eq. A collapse **excess** would favour physical one-outcome modification against simple many-branch Everett unitary dynamics; absence progressively excludes **particular** collapse points, leaving both E and B_eq alive. A verified recoherence after durable record would challenge *irreversible pre-reversal collapse* but not prove ontological branch reality. Quantum eraser/extended Wigner friend is a proposal to extend test domain, NOT observation of an observer in two worlds. Source: 2025 Steane https://doi.org/10.1007/s10701-025-00831-8 ; 2020 Bong et al https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-020-0990-x.

### Gate 4 · MEDIUM: independent observer facts with adversarial Local Friendliness prerequisites

SRC-047 original six-photon experiment (already critically studied) and Bong et al 2020 theory/photonic proof of principle. Explicit prerequisites include locality, experimental choice, and absolute events. Competing 2025 source https://doi.org/10.3390/e27060563 argues no-go theorem scope is weaker. Do NOT score as proof of many histories: a violation rejects a set of auxiliary assumptions and can be consistent with nonlocal B_eq. Upgrade only if a specific one-history model's predicted inequality and all its premises are independently shown to conflict with data.

### Gate 5 · MEDIUM/LOW: primordial nonequilibrium in cosmology

Valentini 2010 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.82.063513 , Underwood & Valentini 2015 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.063531 . Target: preregistered power-spectrum deviations and correlations compared against inflationary physics, instrument systematics and astrophysical nuisance explanations. Initial-condition flexibility is broad and evidence can be degenerate with many non-Bohm cosmological processes. Do not relabel evidence of cosmic bubble regions as Everett branches.

### Deprioritized for central-needle work

Gravitational clock tests, closed timelike curves, speculative travel-to-the-past, circadian clocks, subjective time, generic popular-science literature, diagrams and uncalibrated cosmological 'multiverse' headlines are educational/contextual. They may remain in their nine original discipline rooms and can receive periodic page maintenance, but should NOT take the majority of the research budget while the operational discriminator remains untested.

## 5. What formula blocks accomplished—and what they do not

Our CSL model/math blocks quantify constraints on **irreversible stochastic localization**; these remove specified single-outcome physical dynamics from admissible parameter space, with source assumptions and detector backgrounds. They do **not** differentiate Everett from standard Bohmian equilibrium. This is the bridge to the central question, not a direct branch census. A first truly directional experiment must alter a probability/physical record differently across *defined* one-history vs many-history candidate laws. Priors over model families can make collapse exclusions **conditionally** redistribute support, but this cannot be called measured many-worlds probability.
