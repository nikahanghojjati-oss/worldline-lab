### Sections, claims and methods
- §1: Relative-state approach claims a broader internal-observer description of quantum theory, including a physically closed universe.
- §2: Orthodox Process 1 discontinuous outcome reduction and Process 2 continuous Schrödinger evolution motivate the external-observer difficulty.
- §3: Everett explicitly postulates only universal unitary Process 2 for isolated total systems. That is an assumption of the model, not a measured branch census.
- §4 / eqs.1–3: Composite state |Ψ>=Σij aij |ξi>|ηj>. Given subsystem-one vector |ξk>, conditional subsystem-two relative ket ∝Σj akj|ηj>. Individual subsystem states are not absolute and are correlated by physical interactions. §4 / eqs.4–8: An ideal system–pointer interaction produces displaced apparatus wave-packets continuously; no global projection occurs. The footnote on approximate measurement argues a general weak measurement cannot always be represented by a projection map.
- §5 / eqs.9–24: An observer's memory is a physical record. Unitary system-observer coupling maps eigencomponents into correlated memory states. Repeatability and agreement between communicating observers hold *relative to a given memory record*. All linear components persist in the total wavefunction.
- §5 **note added in proof** (retypeset pp.14–15): Everett directly rejects an extra physical transition from possible to only-one-actual branch; in the model every superposed component is treated as actual. This is the ontology the paper advocates, but it remains an interpretive physical postulate.
- §5 / eqs.25–34: Imposes phase-independent positive additive measure on mutually orthogonal amplitudes. Writing |α|²=Σ|ai|² and m(|α|)=Σm(|ai|), the induced g(x)=m(√x) satisfies g(x+y)=g(x)+g(y); positivity yields m(a)∝|a|². For independent repeated measurements weights of memory records multiply and Born-typical frequencies dominate *by this measure*.
- §5 closing and §6: Common observer records, measurement uncertainties and conventional operational quantum predictions arise in the formalism, under postulates and typicality criteria. External experimental comparison remains with standard quantum mechanics.

### Reproducible bound and adverse critique
Choose a=√0.6, b=√0.4. Normalized weights are 0.6 and 0.4. After two independently correlated ideal observations, strings AA, AB, BA, BB have respective measures 0.36, 0.24, 0.24, 0.16; sum exactly 1. Uniform branch *counting* would yield 0.25 each, so it is not Everett's positive additive measure. This arithmetic does not justify treating branch weights as subjective chances without further decision-theoretic or self-location premises. Branch basis and macroscopic record decomposition are approximate; note Everett's claim predates contemporary environment-selection arguments. Criticism: an empirically indistinguishable Bohmian equilibrium model can have one occupied actual configuration yet the same frequencies; objective-collapse theories depart from unitary dynamics and can be experimentally constrained only under named parameters, backgrounds and statistical protocols.

No independently observed second history and no uniquely discriminating experiment is established by this reading. SRC-012 SEP MWI full study and SRC-010 / SRC-059 Bohm full original studies provide serious comparator. Do not mistake the author's explicit all-branches-actual claim for observed evidence of them.
