# Field Note 004 — Does our universe have one timeline or multiple physically real histories?
Worldline Lab · S-001 · R005 · 21 October 2026

**This is Worldline Lab's central question.** Backward time travel, closed timelike curves, and grandfather paradoxes are secondary questions. A person observing a single sequence of outcomes does not demonstrate that only one history is physically real.

## Precise vocabulary and evidence policy
1. **One observed record:** a person experiences one definite succession of outcomes. All major interpretations have to account for that.
2. **One actual outcome history:** Bohmian theories assign one actual particle configuration; objective-collapse models aim to select one macroscopic outcome by a changed dynamical law.
3. **Many Everett branches:** a *single* universal quantum state may contain many approximately autonomous, decohered macroscopic outcome-relative worlds. Everett's "many" need not mean several independently existing universes or reversible tracks.
4. **Inflationary bubbles:** physically separate cosmological regions in some inflation models, distinct from Everett branches.
5. **CTCs:** speculative global causal structures that neither require nor establish Everett branches.

**Actual study depth:** publisher/institution original abstracts and targeted §§1,2.1,6 of the June 2026 Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy article were examined. None of the complete research papers below was read or replicated end-to-end in this round. We do not promote works to "fully studied" for having catalogued them.

## Research section 1 — Everett's relative-state and branch ontology
[Everett (1957)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454) and [Vaidman, Stanford Encyclopedia (revised 2026)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-manyworlds/), §§1, 2.1 and 6. Everett-type views take the universal quantum state to evolve without special physical collapse. Decohered states are interpreted as many branch-relative macroscopic histories. The distinction between one universal state and multiple experienced worlds is central, and the branch decomposition is approximate. The Born-rule justification and the physical interpretation of branches remain contested. **Competing theory:** Bohm's single actual configuration. **Needed test:** a reproducible observation that cannot be explained equally by both unmodified models. **Present verdict:** no verified observation of an extra actual branch.

## Research section 2 — Decoherence and Schrödinger's cat
[Zurek (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.75.715) and [Schlosshauer (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.76.1267) publisher abstracts. A measurement interaction correlates an outcome with apparatus and environmental states. If the environment records become nearly orthogonal, local interference between outcomes becomes hard to observe: the off-diagonal terms of the subsystem's reduced density matrix become small. That **does not** itself delete the global state or prove that all possible outcomes correspond to physically existing separate histories. Schrödinger's cat illustrates precisely this gap between mathematical state and experienced definiteness. **Next test:** controlled macroscale interference and a quantitative environmental model. **Verdict:** decoherence is not a branch census.

## Research section 3 — Bohm's single-actual-configuration alternative
[Bohm I (1952)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.166) and [Bohm II (1952)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.180), primary abstracts. In Bohmian quantum theory definite particle positions evolve guided by a quantum wave. In quantum equilibrium the observed statistics can match ordinary quantum predictions, while the ontology contains one actual particle configuration. This is not a local hidden-variable model; nonlocal dynamics are essential. **Strong counterpoint:** Bell tests challenge *local* realism, not all definite-history models. **Needed test:** specified non-equilibrium or modified dynamics with a divergent prediction. **Verdict:** familiar quantum observations alone do not eliminate a one-actual-configuration account.

## Research section 4 — Objective collapse and experiments
[Ghirardi, Rimini and Weber (1986)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.34.470) and [Fein et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-019-0663-9), publisher abstracts. GRW changes quantum dynamics with stochastic localizations so macroscopic outcomes become definite under parameters that can, in principle, be experimentally constrained. Large-molecule interference demonstrates that coherence survives in increasingly complex physical systems and constrains portions of certain modification parameter spaces. **Critical caveat:** an interference experiment does not confirm a visible Everett world, nor exclude all physical-collapse parameter values. **Needed test:** detailed comparison of observed visibility, background decoherence and predicted collapse-induced losses. **Verdict:** a potentially discriminating program, not a resolved count.

## Research section 5 — Loophole-free Bell tests
[Bell (1964)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysicsPhysiqueFizika.1.195), [Hensen et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature15759), and [Shalm et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.250402), primary publisher/institution abstracts. Hensen's distant-spin test reported 245 trials, CHSH S=2.42 ± 0.20 and a local-realist-null p value at most 0.039. Shalm's independent photon test also strongly rejected an appropriate local-realist null with separated settings and efficient detectors. Bell violations are important observations of quantum correlation, but **they do not count histories**; Bohmian mechanics is nonlocal, and differing quantum interpretations reproduce the core probabilities. **Needed test:** specify an ontology-sensitive physical prediction rather than voting for many worlds based on local-realism rejection. **Verdict:** not a direct discriminator.

## Research section 6 — Cosmological bubbles are not quantum branches
[Feeney et al. (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.071301) and [Feeney et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.083515), publisher abstracts. Some models of eternal inflation include multiple bubble domains. Collisions might create characteristic patterns in the cosmic microwave background (CMB). A WMAP 7-year search did not warrant adding bubble collision patterns to the standard model; later work forecast conditional detection capability with temperature/polarization information. This is neither proof that all bubbles are impossible nor evidence for Everett histories. **Needed test:** independently reproduce a specific sky-data bubble-template comparison and test it against updated CMB measurements. **Verdict:** a separate, unresolved cosmological question.

## Research section 7 — Quantum-state reality is not branch count
[Pusey, Barrett and Rudolph (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys2309), publisher abstract, and SEP 2026. Under preparation-independence assumptions, some purely epistemic quantum-state models conflict with quantum predictions. This does **not** entail multiple actual outcome histories: Bohmian and Everettian accounts can each treat a quantum state seriously while differing over which macroscopic outcomes are actual. **Needed test:** independently check theorem assumptions and seek predictions separating physical history ontologies.

## The result and next research gate
Our **central empirical answer remains unresolved**. Experiments establish quantum phenomena and constrain specific alternatives; they do not yet directly observe and count branch-relative worlds.
