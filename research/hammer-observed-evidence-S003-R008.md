## Scientific problem, result, and verdict

The project asks if the physical universe contains **one actually realized history** or **multiple physically realized histories**. HAMMER searched for authentic experimentally published, operationally discriminating observations rather than inventions of models or artificial score changes. Three primary experimental papers now support **three new, precisely scoped scientific Ledger movements** (MOVE-010 through MOVE-012, linked to WL-023 through WL-025). These are real, source-reported experimental observations. However, they **do not produce differing measured likelihoods between Everett and quantum-equilibrium Bohmian mechanics**. The central editorial Compass remains **50**, which is not an empirical 50% probability.

This is deliberate: model-class evidence changes when its experimental predictions survive or fail, even though more fundamental ontology remains observationally unresolved. An inference from disproving Bell-local models to disproving **all** one-history models would be invalid because explicitly nonlocal one-history alternatives exist. Likewise, coherent quantum interference disfavors sufficiently strong *specific collapse laws*, but quantum-equilibrium Bohmian mechanics gives the same interference.

## 1. MOVE-010 · Real 2026 metallic nanoparticle matter-wave interference

**Original:** Sebastian Pedalino, Bruno E. Ramírez-Galindo, Richard Ferstl, Klaus Hornberger, Markus Arndt and Stefan Gerlich, *Probing quantum mechanics with nanoparticle matter-wave interferometry*, Nature **649**, 866–870 (published **January 21, 2026**), DOI **10.1038/s41586-025-09917-9**; new source **SRC-081** and critical targeted note **NOTE-072**. Original experiment data and code: [Zenodo 17502163](https://zenodo.org/records/17502163), archive `Na-Cluster-Interference.zip` containing `Macroscopicity_April2025.py`, `talbotlau.py` and original cluster counts. Repository identity and contents verified; the ZIP could not be downloaded during this round, so **the authors' raw 3,895-data-point Bayesian fit was not independently rerun**.

**Observed physical fact:** Source reports coherent fringes from sodium metal nanoparticles (more than 7,000 atoms, characteristic mass ~172 kDa) with two fitted representative scan visibilities **0.10 ± 0.01** and **0.08 ± 0.01**. Original Methods describe optical Talbot–Lau interferometry, standing-wave period **133 nm**, three grating separation **0.983 m**, and a cluster beam spanning **120–170 m/s**. The experiment studies spatial delocalization on a scale well exceeding particle diameter, not measurement of another conscious observer's separate Everett branch.

**HAMMER reproducible arithmetic from original published numerical values:**
- From `L ≈ d²/λdB` and `λdB=h/(mv)`, at `m=172000 Da`, `d=133 nm`, `L=0.983 m`, the nominal first-Talbot resonance speed is `v≈L h/(m d²) ≈129 m/s`, within the paper's source beam range 120–170 m/s. This is an **approximate geometry consistency check**, not an independent Fourier fringe fit.
- The authors' 3,895 analysis data points yield a reported **lower 5% posterior classicalization-time quantile** `τm=2.84×10^15 seconds`. Their declared macroscopicity `μ=log10(τm/1 s)` gives **μ≈15.453**, reproducing published μ≈15.45 from a second mathematical pathway. The posterior was fitted by the original authors under their **minimal macrorealist modification family and Bayesian prior**, not by HAMMER.
- The amplitude/quoted fit-error ratios of 10 and 8 are informal checks of reported scan-fit precision **not rigorous global detection significance**, and may share systematics.

**Direction that moves:** Evidence disfavors source-defined *minimal macrorealist modifications* that would suppress these interferometric fringes beyond the reported posterior constraint. **Does not move:** probability that Everett's multiple ontic branches versus Bohmian one actual configuration exists. Both standard frameworks reproduce ordinary Born interference. The source's posterior is not a universal probability of all single-world theories being false.

**New Ledger:** WL-023, MOVE-010. Reproduction code `tools/hammer/observed-evidence.cjs`.

## 2. MOVE-011 · Real 4.32-billion-trial loophole-free Hardy test

**Original:** Si-Ran Zhao and colleagues, *Loophole-Free Test of Local Realism via Hardy's Violation*, Physical Review Letters **133**, 060201 (published **August 7, 2024**), DOI **10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.060201**; new source **SRC-082**, targeted note **NOTE-073**. APS original published abstract and available original full-text excerpt were checked, **not the entire supplement and event-level setting/outcome records**.

**Source reported observed physical fact:** Entangled photons and random setting switches in a six-hour run of **4.32×10^9 trials**, detector efficiency **82.2%**, fidelity **99.10%** and `P_Hardy=4.646×10^-4`. Authors report a local-realistic null-hypothesis **p upper bound of 10^-16348** under their experimental design, null-test and loophole assumptions. HAMMER records the enormous p-value **as log10 p≤−16348**, without numeric floating underflow or inventing a likelihood ratio. That number **was not rederived from original event-level data**, so it retains authorship and model scope.

**Direction that moves:** Strong experimental exclusion of the tested **Bell-local and measurement-independent hidden-variable outcome class**. **Not excluded:** nonlocal one-actual-history approaches, including standard quantum-equilibrium Bohmian mechanics, or the actual physical existence of Everett branches. A frequentist null p-value is **not** a posterior `P(one world|data)`, a Bayes factor, or proof of reality of multiple observed outcomes.

**New Ledger:** WL-024, MOVE-011.

## 3. MOVE-012 · October 2026 quantum reservoir irreversibility experiment

**Original:** Ri-Hua Zheng and colleagues, *Observation of the Transition from Reversible to Irreversible Decoherence of Mesoscopic Quantum Superpositions*, Physical Review Letters **137**, 150201 (published **October 5, 2026**), DOI **10.1103/t2yx-9bqh**, new source **SRC-083**, abstract-level note **NOTE-074**.

**Author-reported experimental fact:** A circuit-QED bus resonator containing a photonic cat is coupled to a controllable reservoir of nonlinear oscillators. Increasing the effective bath degrees of freedom progressively diminishes the **spontaneously erasable which-path information**; for sufficiently many coupled modes, no natural later interaction restores it under the experimental protocol. The paper investigates non-Markovianity. Access in this round was the original publisher **abstract only**; no original full-text figures, calibrations or quantitative bath scaling were independently reproduced.

**Direction that moves:** Actual controlled-bath experiments support a **specified practical loss of spontaneous coherence recoverability** and show that reservoir structure must be modeled, rather than equating reduced-state irreversibility with fundamental physical collapse. **Does not establish:** an irreversible collapse of the universe's global quantum state, a physically singled-out real timeline, or additional real Everett branches.

**New Ledger:** WL-025, MOVE-012.

## 4. Evidence-partition theorem and why adding unrelated p-values would be wrong

For two fully specified rival physical models `H1` (Everettian Born record statistics) and `H2` (equilibrium Bohmian Born record statistics), if the measurement protocol `d` gives the *same entire joint predictive distribution* `P(D|H1,d)=P(D|H2,d)`, the likelihood ratio `P(D|H1,d)/P(D|H2,d)=1` on every positive-probability observed record. Thus **no correctly performed Bayesian update from those common observations changes their prior odds**; source-only claims about collapse/hidden-variable subclasses cannot be carried over to every single-history theory without an additional tested physical premise. This is a straightforward established likelihood/equivalence identity, verified in earlier project's R012, **not novel information theory**.

**HAMMER's useful engineered decision:** a source is admitted as an evidence movement only for a named falsifiable physical claim whose assumptions the observation actually tests. We introduce **three separate measured-source movement records** with explicit original study, claim, direction, test statistic/effect when available, source reading depth, reproducibility status and `centralScoreImpact:0`. The broader ontology verdict stays unresolved. The value is resisting false inferences while **really updating the models**. None of the three is multiplied to manufacture some huge many-worlds posterior.
