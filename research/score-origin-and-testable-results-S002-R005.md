### Actual experimental method and observed result

- Physical measured channel: low-energy electron-recoil data from the first XENONnT science run, July 6–November 10, 2021; exposure **1.16 tonne-years** inside a 4.37±0.14 tonne fiducial mass; search electron recoil range **1–140 keV**, emitted X-rays predicted by Markovian CSL and Diósi–Penrose collapse dynamics (pp.120201-2 to -4).
- The model recognizes xenon electron–proton charge cancellation at low X-ray photon energies. The detector response uses energy smearing, efficiency and **nine background components**; an **unbinned maximum-likelihood** fit compares the model plus background to data. Nuisance details and assumptions matter: different detector efficiency assumptions change the model limits ~17% (CSL) / ~6% (DP) in the published main discussion (p.120201-4).
- Authors report **no statistically significant spontaneous-radiation excess**, CSL local discovery 0.3σ, and **90% confidence upper limit λ_CSL / r_C² < 3.0×10⁻³ s⁻¹ m⁻²** (95% CL 3.7×10⁻³) for their *Markovian white-noise CSL radiation model* (p.120201-4). Compared with earlier Majorana, 90% bound is reported ~135 times stronger, not merely a vague "no update".
- For the benchmark r_C = 10⁻⁷ m and λ_CSL = 10⁻¹⁶ s⁻¹ (a parameter pair associated with the historical GRW proposal when imported into **CSL**), the ratio λ/r_C² = 10⁻² s⁻¹ m⁻²; it exceeds the new 90%-CL upper bound by a factor of about 3.33. Paper's full profile-likelihood comparison reports this **CSL parameter pair 9.1σ from best fit**. That reported model-specific statistic is not computed from 3.33 alone, and cannot be reproduced independently without data/likelihood. **Not** proof that the original discrete GRW 1986 stochastic-hit model is fully excluded: authors' test applies to Markovian CSL benchmark dynamics.
- Diósi–Penrose lower cutoff **R₀ > 4.9×10⁻¹⁰ m at 90% CL** (95% 4.5×10⁻¹⁰ m) under the paper's specific model, with local discovery significance ~0.2σ (p.120201-5). White-noise CSL bounds leave colored/dissipative versions open, and do not establish physical existence of Everett branches.

## Program redesign: pursue answers rather than reading for its own sake

For every 1–2 primary-source research rounds: start with a *precisely testable hypothesis*; record the null and alternative; derive expected difference in a particular measurement; find/inspect published raw observations or model-specific constraints; quantify the effect size, uncertainty and competing noise processes; state what the result actually rules out, what remains possible and why; update a claim-specific evidence status. A paper read is an input, not an answer. If two models are operationally identical by construction, mark the pair "**not identified by current experiment class**" and stop rewarding repetitive non-discriminating studies. Move to proposals with measurable differences (objective collapse, pilot-wave nonequilibrium under explicit altered assumptions, empirically viable distinctions); keep the ontological question separate and any surviving identifiability theorem honest.

Future movement protocol:
- **Claim ledger always advances** when specific evidence narrows an allowable physical-model region, refutes a falsifiable hypothesis, improves confidence or overturns an error. It must include prior state, measured new state, confidence, sources, controls and limitations. Do not invent generic numerical weights.
- **Central directional dial moves** only if an experiment's reported distribution differs for one actual vs genuinely many actual outcome-histories *under matched, independently defensible assumptions*, with quantified likelihood and independent alternatives. This may not become identifiable using current formulations and should never be promised on a timetable.
- A negative experimental result can change the model-specific ledger without changing which metaphysical ontology is favored. That distinction is scientific success, not stagnation.
